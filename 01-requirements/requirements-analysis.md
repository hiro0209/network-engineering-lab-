# Requirements Analysis

## 1. Purpose
The purpose of this document is to identify and define the network requirements needed to support ABC Pty Ltd's current business operations and future growth. These requirements are derived from the RFP and customer interview and provide a clear foundation for the network architecture and design.


## 2. Business Requirements

### BR-001 — 
The company must have a new network infrastructure to support its business operations.

### BR-002 — 
The network must support current business requirements while allowing the organisation to expand in the future.

### BR-003 — 
The company must have a reliable Internet connection.

### BR-004 — 
The company must protect its business resources and information from unauthorised access.

## 3. User Requirements

### UR-001 — Employee Access to Internal Resources
Employees need secure access to internal company resources.

### UR-002 — Employee Access for Business Activities
Employees need to access internal resources to perform their work.

### UR-003 — Network and Access Management
IT administrators need to manage the company's network and access permissions appropriately.

### UR-004 — Network Configuration
IT administrators need to configure the network when required.

### UR-005 — Network Monitoring and Troubleshooting
IT administrators need to monitor the network regularly and troubleshoot issues when they occur.

### UR-006 — Guest Wi-Fi Access
Guests and visitors need access to the guest Wi-Fi.

### UR-007 — Remote Access
Remote users need secure remote access to internal company resources.
